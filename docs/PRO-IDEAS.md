**90-second demo**

Preload the room and four memories. Treat these timings as rehearsal targets.

- **0–12 seconds:** Show one selected trip photo. Ask a specific unresolved question: “Why did we change our route that day?”
- **12–35:** Show the agent navigating one selected email conversation through its actual UI. It distinguishes the original plan from a later correction and opens the supporting message.
- **35–55:** Reveal the recovered detail alongside its source excerpt and screenshot. Add it to the fifth anchor, beside a recognizable room feature.
- **55–75:** Walk the five-stop route. Each stop contains one photo or short clip and one sentence that advances the story.
- **75–90:** Hide the cards. Have the presenter return to the relevant anchor and retell the recovered detail, then reveal the source again.

Choose the trip because its material contains a compelling detail. A booking confirmation alone will make this feel like administrative search.

**Biggest objection: “This is a photo album inside an arbitrary room.”**

That objection is valid. The repeatable route and return-to-location interaction must earn the spatial presentation. They demonstrate spatial use; they do not establish improved memory. With a licensed room, Gaussian splats remain a presentation choice. Personal-place reconstruction is an eventual justification you cannot claim today.

**First-30-minute kill test**

Require one real app-UI lookup to recover a meaningful detail, preserve supporting evidence, and deliver it into a clickable viewer anchor. A teammate must understand why that detail belongs in the story. Failure kills that source workflow or story choice. A fixture does not pass.

**Three lanes**

**A:** Own the viewer, five-stop route, media reveals, and final recall interaction.

**B:** Own importing the licensed splat, camera framing, and reliable placement coordinates. Spend this lane on scene quality; reconstruction is outside this demo.

**C:** Own the selected app workflow, supported interpretation, and evidence attached to the recovered memory.

**Cuts:** New reconstruction stack, multiple source apps, dense legacy software without story relevance, semantic splat editing, custom training, headset support, and broad camera-roll ingestion.
